# Approval and locking are states of each Work Entry, not of a period

Most time-tracking systems group hours into a Timesheet, approve that, and close a period once it is invoiced. We don't. Each Work Entry is Pending, Approved or Locked on its own. Approving or locking many entries at once, such as a Project's month, is only a bulk action over the entries selected at that moment. Locking skips Pending entries and closes no period, so a Work Entry recorded later for a locked month starts Pending and is approved and locked in a later run. This keeps Work Entries free of any grouping (see the glossary) and lets late or corrected hours flow through without reopening anything already invoiced. Don't introduce Timesheets or closed periods without revisiting this.
